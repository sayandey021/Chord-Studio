## 3. Full Store Description
*(Formatted for Microsoft Store Storefront)*

```text
Chord Studio is a modern, high-performance guitar tablature viewer, interactive chord progression analyzer, precision chromatic instrument tuner, and on-device AI transcription suite built natively for Windows.

Designed from the ground up for acoustic guitarists, electric shredders, bassists, ukulele players, and songwriters, Chord Studio delivers a fluid, distraction-free environment to practice, learn, transcribe, and perform your favorite songs with zero ads and complete offline privacy.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎸 PRECISION TAB VIEWER & INTERACTIVE CHORDS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Monospace Reader: Crystal-clear monospace layout with syntax-highlighted chord markers, verse sections, and solos.
• Instant Interactive Chord Popups: Click or tap any chord name to inspect high-definition fretboard fingering diagrams and 61-key piano voicings across standard, barre, 7th, maj7, suspended, and diminished variations.
• Real-Time Transpose Engine: Transpose any chord progression up or down ±12 semitones instantly with intelligent enharmonic pitch correction.
• Capo Calculator: Automatically recalibrate fingering positions to match your capo placement.
• 60 FPS Subpixel Autoscroll: Hands-free practicing with adjustable playback scrolling speed (0.5x to 3.0x) and quick pause-on-click controls.
• Multi-Instrument Voicings: Switch effortlessly between 6-String Guitar, 7-String Guitar, Ukulele, and Bass voicings.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🤖 AI AUDIO TO TAB & SYNCED KARAOKE STUDIO
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• On-Device Neural Chord Recognition: Import any MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, or OGG track and let on-device ONNX neural models transcribe root chords and timing directly from the audio.
• Subharmonic CQT DSP Engine: Advanced frequency-to-chroma signal processing isolates bass fundamentals and suppresses harmonic noise.
• Synced Lyrics & Karaoke: Import or transcribe time-aligned lyrics with interactive seeking and live floating lyric playback.
• Visual Multi-Track Waveform: Scrub through audio with millisecond accuracy, synchronized chord timeline markers, and customizable loop points.
• Local Privacy: Neural inference runs 100% locally on your PC. Your audio files are never uploaded to remote cloud servers.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎙️ HIGH-PRECISION CHROMATIC TUNER
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Live Pitch Detection: High-precision autocorrelation and YIN pitch tracking with live needle deviation (-50 to +50 cents).
• Karplus-Strong Physical Synthesis: Click any headstock string peg to hear authentic wooden acoustic plucks and reference pitch tones.
• Tuning Presets: Out-of-the-box support for Standard (EADGBE), Drop D, Half-Step Down (Eb), DADGAD, Open D, Open G, Open C, Drop C, 7-String Guitar, Ukulele (Standard, Low G, D-Tuning, Baritone), 4 & 5-String Bass, Banjo, and Violin/Mandolin.
• Configurable Hardware: Select any microphone or USB audio interface directly from Studio settings.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
🎓 LEARN & THEORY STUDIO (CHORDS, SCALES & STRUMMING)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Chord Encyclopedia: Master every chord shape across Guitar, Ukulele, Piano, and Bass with multiple CAGED voicings, fretboard markers, and instant audio preview.
• Multi-Instrument Scale Lab: Explore 12+ major, minor, pentatonic, blues, and modal scales with interactive 15-fret fretboard mapping and piano keyboards.
• Strumming Rhythm Master: Learn essential strumming patterns across 4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 2/4, 12/8, and 5/4 time signatures with animated beat cursors and synthesized metronome playback.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
📄 STUDIO PDF EXPORTER & SHEET MUSIC
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Vector PDF Generation: Export clean, beautifully typeset A4 chord sheets ready for physical gig binders or digital tablets.
• Preserves Structure: Automatic page break formatting, capo annotations, and artist metadata preservation.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
💾 OFFLINE-FIRST LIBRARY & PRIVACY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
• Zero Latency Offline Storage: Saved tabs, custom creations, favorites, and playlists are stored locally using SQLite.
• Complete JSON Backup & Restore: Export and import your entire song library anytime with standard common dialogs.
• Zero Ads & Zero Tracking: No telemetry, no forced sign-ins, and no banner ads to disrupt your music sessions.
```
